Tilt Up Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Tilt Up handles information when you play and use its online features.

Request deletion of your Tilt Up data

Information used by the game

Tilt Up uses a randomly assigned player ID and an anonymous session to recognize your game profile. The online service stores your leaderboard nickname, scores, weekly run and reward records, and daily reward progress. Your nickname and ranked scores can be visible to other players. Please avoid putting personal information in your nickname.

The game stores session information, preferences, unlocked items, selections, and local progress on your device.

Why we use this information

We use it to maintain your game profile, display rankings, track reward eligibility, and prevent duplicate reward claims. Supabase hosts the authentication and database services and processes the information needed to provide these features. The app connects to these services over HTTPS.

Delete your Tilt Up account and data

Profile, score, and reward records are kept to support ongoing play. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes its local data but does not send a deletion request for online records.

  1. If you can still open your player profile in Tilt Up, go to Settings → Delete my data and choose Send request. The request is tied to your current player session.
  2. If you cannot access the game or your profile, email ramedusastudios.support@gmail.com with your in-game nickname, if known. Include your player ID if you previously saved it. We may need more information to verify the request before deleting data. Never send passwords or session tokens.

When we process a verified request, we delete the online account, profile and nickname, leaderboard scores, weekly runs and reward records, and daily reward progress from the active game database. Preferences and other local game data can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.
